Dragging and throwing — both themes

Light theme — cursor repulsion

Repels characters away from the cursor. The force softens near the cursor and reaches zero at the radius.

Dark theme — cursor attraction

Attracts characters toward the cursor. The force softens near the cursor and reaches zero at the radius.

Pass-through attraction

In dark theme, characters near the cursor temporarily stop feeling attraction so they can fly past. Attraction returns after the cooldown and once they are at least 1.5× the release distance away. Light-theme repulsion stays continuous.

Saved settings apply to new page loads, including the private debug preview. Saving does not turn the public effect on.
